Two homes, one camera

Photography began for me as a way to capture a scene. Increasingly, I'm interested in whether we can capture enough of a place to step back inside it.

I've always enjoyed photographing places, especially the details that make an environment feel inhabited rather than merely picturesque. Over time that expanded from conventional photography into 360° capture and spatial media — a single photograph preserves a viewpoint; a spatial capture tries to preserve the possibility of looking around. That thread continues more technically in the Lab's digital-twin experiments; here, it's just about the places themselves.
