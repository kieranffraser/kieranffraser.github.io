Photogrammetry

Two early scans, made from ordinary phone photos rather than any proper scanning rig.

These came from the same period as my Tilt Brush experiments, but they turned out to matter more than they seemed to at the time: one scanned my desk in Trinity's Lloyd building from about 110 phone photos; the other scanned a mug my dad, Barry, made for me on his pottery wheel, from around 40 photos processed in Reality Capture. Long before the wedding pottery project, that mug was the first time I'd pointed a camera at something he'd made and tried to capture it properly.

The models below are live Sketchfab embeds — drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
