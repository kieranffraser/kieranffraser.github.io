Summary

A researcher and engineer working across AI, security and human-computer interaction. I graduated from the University of Dublin, Trinity College with a PhD in Computer Science for research on synthetic data generation and push-notification technology (ETHOS Push). I then spent two years applying that research to commercial use as a Research Assistant at Trinity's ADAPT Centre, publishing jointly at leading Mobile, HCI and NLP conferences. From 2022 I was a Research Engineer at IBM on the Security and Privacy of AI team, working on adversarial robustness and red-teaming for vision and large-language models. Since November 2026 I've been a Staff Machine Learning Engineer at PayPal, working on onboarding and early-lifecycle fraud detection.
